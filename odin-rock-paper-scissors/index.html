Refresh the Page if you haven't received any prompts or you didn't finish 5 rounds of the game for some reason

  1. F12 to open Developer Tools and Head over to the Console Tab
    OR
    Ctrl+Shift+J (Windows/Linux), Cmd+Option+J (macOS) to directly access Console.
  2. After finishing 5 rounds (which you probably did if you're seeing this and no more popups), Console is where the scores will be printed.
